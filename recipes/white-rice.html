Chicken Teriyaki Recipe

Description

Here's how to make rice if you're like... stupid I guess :3

Ingredients

  • Jasmine rice (who's jasmine???)

Steps

  1. Measure out 1.5 cups of white rice into your rice-cooker's bowl.
  2. Rinse and agitate your rice with cold water until the water is mostly clear.
  3. Fill your rice-cooker's bowl to the "2" line.
  4. Put the bowl into your rice cooker and turn it on, then wait for it to finish
