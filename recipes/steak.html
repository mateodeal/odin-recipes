Chicken Teriyaki Recipe

Description

This is my favorite way to cook a New York strip in an air fryer. I hope you enjoy!

Ingredients

  • A New York Strip
  • Various spices of your choosing.

Steps

  1. Put spices on the steak (duh)
  2. Throw that jawn in the air fryer for like 3-4 minutes at 350-400 degrees.
  3. Flip her over and let her cook for a couple more minutes.
  4. Pull her out and let her rest for a few minutes before eating her up.
